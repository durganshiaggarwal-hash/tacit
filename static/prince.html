Visual Inspection Standard: Cast Pump Impeller

Foundry line · final visual inspection before machining

1. Scope

Applies to every cast impeller leaving the foundry. Inspect the top face under bench lighting.

2. Procedure

  1. Clean the casting and place it face up.
  2. Inspect the sealing lip, flange face and outer rim for pits.
  3. Measure the largest pit with a gauge.
  4. Decide accept or reject using section 3. Record rejects.
