Every plant has rules its best people never wrote down. When they retire, the rules go with them.

TACIT finds those rules without an interview. The master only says yes or no to parts, sounds and strokes that TACIT generates, each one picked where their judgment is most likely to differ from the manual. Then it trains juniors on exactly the rules they are missing.

How it works

Five steps, in order. The master spends minutes, not days; everything after step 2 runs without them.

  1. Capture the master熟練者の判定

    TACIT generates a part, a pump sound or a deburring stroke. The master accepts or rejects it. Each next case is chosen where the master's line is still unclear and most likely to differ from the manual.

    The masterAbout 10 minutes per sense

  2. Rule cardsルールカード

    Every place the master's line differs from the manual becomes a plain rule, in English and Japanese, with real examples on both sides. The master approves it, rewords it or strikes it out.

    The masterA few minutes

  3. Train a junior若手を育てる

    Practice cases are generated just past the master's line, on the side this junior gets wrong. A rule only counts as learned when they call it right on both sides of the line.

    The junior10 to 40 practice cases

  4. The Prince's Copy書き込み入りの標準書

    The written standard with the master's corrections in the margin: ink where the master approved, pencil where they have not yet.

    EveryoneThe new reference copy

  5. Skill Passport技能パスポート

    Which of the master's rules each junior holds, which are certified and signed off, and which still live in one person's head.
