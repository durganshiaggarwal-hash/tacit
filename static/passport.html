Skill Passport技能パスポート

Plant overview

What one master knows that is not written down, how much of it has been captured, and how much of it a junior already holds.

The master and junior in this demo are simulated unless you answered yourself. Capture minutes are estimates (about 5 s per casting, 8 s per sound clip, 7 s per stroke). A real deployment would tie each passport to a named person and plant.
